# A9 – Pulleys

## Objective
The objective of this assignment is to design a pulley system to transfer power between two parallel axes. the requirement I've chosen for the assignment were a center distance between the two pulleys of 5 inches, a pulley diameter of 3 inches, rotational speed of 2000 rpm, belt tension of 5 lbf under stationary conditions, the pulley to be made from mild steel with a friction coefficient of 0.5, and a minimum safety factor of 3/\.

## Shaft design
To find the diameter of the shaft, the torsion can be rearranged to solve for the diameter. The shaft is made from 1060 hardened steel that has a yield strength of 89.9 ksi. The calculated diameter of the shaft was 1.588 inches, and with an RC5 class fit, the tolerance on the hole is +1.6 thousandths of an inch for the hole and -2.0 or -3.0 thousandths of an inch for the shaft.

## Lessons learned
Through this assignment, I learned how values and parameters carry over from one component to the next in a system. For example, the belt tensions determine the loads on the shaft, which in turn determine the reactions at the bearings. Because this was a pulley system, I also practiced applying torsion correctly, since the shaft transmits torque from the difference between the belt tensions. I learned that once the system is in motion, the tensions on the two sides of the belt are no longer equal, and that difference is what produces the transmitted torque.
