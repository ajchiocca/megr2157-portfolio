# A3 – Parametric and FEA

## Objective
The objective of this assignment was to design bar of a circular cross section in CAD software to simulate its deflection from an applied load using finite element analysis. The bars deflection, area, applied force, and modulus of elasticity were determined beforehand to estimate the length given the material and shape the bar will be in: The applied direct load must be between 300 to 500 pounds, max axial deflection must be 0.009 inches, and the modulus of elasticity must be between 8.5 E6 to 11.5 E6 KSI.
